# Dverity Code of Conduct

[中文版](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md) | [English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Dverity welcomes direct, rigorous technical debate. Discussion must stay
focused on code, design, evidence, and maintainability rather than people.

Contributors must:

- challenge claims with reproducible evidence;
- explain risks and trade-offs without insults or harassment;
- respect privacy, security embargoes, and explicit authority boundaries;
- accept maintainers' decisions on repository scope and moderation;
- avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or personally demeaning conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately through the least public contact on the
[maintainer profile](https://github.com/Dimon94). Maintainers may edit, reject,
hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary.
